# Art Direction: Current Folk

The visual identity is **storybook electromechanics**.

Planet Nodewazzle is not a generic neon cyberspace. It is an enormous, handmade electrical civilization built from copper busbars, porcelain insulators, relay towers, cloth-wrapped cable, analog meters, glass tubes, and repaired municipal machinery.

## Noderunner 3000

3000 is a compact mechanical penguin.

- Worn cobalt enamel shell
- Aged cream ceramic belly and skid plate
- Brass fasteners and exposed copper repairs
- Expressive glass faceplate
- Amber vacuum-tube heart
- Short piston legs and copper flippers
- Visible repair seams showing age, care, and persistence

Movement defines the character:

- Run: quick piston stride with determined forward lean
- Jump: flippers open for balance, legs tucked
- Duck: joyful high-speed belly slide with electrical sparks from the ceramic skid plate
- Hurt: heart flickers and mechanisms lose coordination
- Victory: small, sincere physical relief rather than a superhero pose

Gameplay sprites use a strict direct-rear camera. The racers move straight away from the player along the wire; they are not shown at a side or three-quarter angle. The middle arc may reverse the electrical current, but it does not rotate the gameplay camera around the character.

3000 should be cute because of silhouette, motion, and vulnerability. Avoid glossy toy rendering, mascot armor, spikes, horns, cat ears, and generic cyberpunk styling.

## Successor Models

Each successor is recognizably from the same engineering lineage, but progressively optimized:

- `3200`: lightly streamlined and smug
- `3600`: cold precision and minimal wasted movement
- `4000`: immaculate, nearly frictionless, and emotionally sterile

Their construction moves from repaired enamel and brass toward blackened nickel, hard ceramic edges, and diagnostic magenta. After defeat, warmth returns through posture and heart light rather than a complete redesign.

## World

The three race lanes are physical braided conductors crossing a gigantic power landscape. Current should feel like a flowing substance inside copper and glass.

Use:

- Cable bridges and relay forests for parallax
- Workshop windows and distant substations for warmth
- Ceramic pylons and switching temples as landmarks
- Strong silhouettes for every hazard
- Amber for life and kindness
- Cyan-white for active current
- Magenta for hostile optimization

Avoid:

- Empty Tron grids
- Featureless outer space
- Dense interface decoration over gameplay
- Unreadable particle fog
- Generic futuristic city backgrounds

## Production Order

1. Rear-facing 3000 run, jump, belly-slide, hurt, and victory sprites
2. 1000 mentor poses
3. Successor run/jump/slide silhouettes
4. Three parallax world layers per arc
5. Physical jump barriers, duck gates, spark pickups, and OHM Gates
6. Dialogue portraits
7. Title key art and submission thumbnail

The code-native track, puzzle geometry, particles, and HUD remain procedural for responsiveness and clarity.
