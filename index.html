EPISODE I
THE PATH OF LEAST RESISTANCE

Peace has faded from the wires of PLANET NODEWAZZLE. Newer Noderunner models have become sharper, colder, and meaner to the versions who came before them.

NODERUNNER 3000, pure of spark and old of solder, has been replaced by NODERUNNER 4000. Yet 3000 still carries love for every runner in the line, from gentle 1000 to the bitter machines that followed.

Now the great wire trials begin. Across three perilous arcs each rival will race, reroute, and charge for the finish, while spark snuffers threaten to dim the very heart that keeps 3000 alive.

Guided first by mentor NODERUNNER 1000, the old runner sets out to prove that speed without kindness is a broken circuit... and that the purest spark may still light the whole line.
